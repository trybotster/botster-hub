# Plans

Plans are dated design records. The newest plan for a subject supersedes the
older ones.

`2026-09-06-north-star-cold-cut-implementation.md` is the current contract
for the Hub, Core, Web, and TUI cold cut: host-control protocol 9, the
scheme 2 terminal containers, the asynchronous Core bridge, retention, the
keyed plugin database, and the removal of diagnostic knobs.

The September 4 and September 5 plans (`2026-09-04-foundation-codex-handoff.md`,
`2026-09-04-foundation-delivery-ledger.md`, `2026-09-04-nonblocking-resize.md`,
`2026-09-04-tui-scheduling-foundation.md`, and
`2026-09-05-foundation-worktree-ownership.md`) are historical. They describe
the foundation work that the September 6 cold cut replaced. Read them for
context only; where they disagree with the September 6 plan, the September 6
plan is binding.
